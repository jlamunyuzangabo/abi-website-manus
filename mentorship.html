Age Bridge Initiative

Mentorship

Experienced professionals are matched with students and early-career professionals, with a focus on management and entrepreneurship.

How matching works

Matches are made on skills, experience, personality and aspirations. Practical toolkits and assessment tools support every mentoring pair.

[Photo: mentoring session (to be supplied)]

Four pillars

Capacity building

Personal growth, life skills and technical skills so mentees can adapt, build their careers and take up opportunities.

Knowledge transfer

Tailored guidance, advice, networking and practical support from seasoned professionals, in a safe space to share ideas and challenges.

Inclusive excellence

Diversity, equity, inclusion and belonging, so every participant feels valued and supported.

Accountability and impact

Progress is tracked, feedback is gathered, and results are measured against agreed KPIs.

Four stages of each mentoring pair

Ice breaking

Mentor and mentee introduce themselves, discuss expectations, build trust and identify objectives.

Navigating

The mentee gets clear on how their goals connect to learning and career options, and sets SMART goals.

Advising

The mentor helps the mentee weigh options and make informed decisions.

Feedback and celebration

The mentor gives regular feedback and recognizes progress and achievements.

Activities include weekly "highs and lows", problem-solving sessions, coaching and project-based learning.
