Why ABI exists

Close the gap. Build the response.

The gap

Poverty persists, especially for women, youth and vulnerable groups. Aid reaching lower-income countries is out of proportion to the results it produces.

Two reasons are given. Institutions have weak capacity to absorb resources. And there are too few skilled people to deliver results on time.

The response

ABI sets out to improve the performance of people, especially youth, and of institutions. It unlocks existing skills and expertise, strengthens institutional capacity, and connects generations.
